Privacy Policy for Curio

Last updated: March 15, 2026

Overview

Curio ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal collection management application. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address and password when you create an account
  • Collection data: Photos, titles, descriptions, and metadata you add to your collections

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: Device type, operating system version
  • Log data: App crash reports and error logs to improve reliability

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the Curio service
  • Sync your collections across devices
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

AI Image Analysis

When you use the AI-powered analysis feature, your photos are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. Google processes this data according to the Google AI Terms. Photos sent for analysis are not used to train AI models. You can always skip AI analysis and enter details manually.

Data Storage

  • Photos and collection data are stored securely in cloud storage (Supabase) with HTTPS encryption
  • A local cache is stored on your device for offline access
  • Public sample collections are visible to all users; your private collections are only accessible to you

Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Privacy Policy
Supabase Database & authentication supabase.com/privacy
Google Gemini AI image analysis ai.google.dev/terms
Vercel Web hosting & analytics vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your photos for AI training

We may share data with the service providers listed above and if required by law.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data through the app
  • Export your collections using the Export feature
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data including collections, items, and photos are permanently removed within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Curio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or via email.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, reach us at privacy@curio.app.
